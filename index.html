      Recommended week

        Indoor tips: use a fan, drink every 15 min, and when your heart rate drifts up late in a ride, lower the resistance rather than leave the zone. General guidance for a healthy adult building aerobic base. If you're new to exercise or have a heart condition, check with a doctor first.

        Profile and settings

        Tuned for a stationary bike: estimated max HR is 5 bpm lower than for running. If you've hit a higher max on the bike, choose "I know my max HR". Zones are % of max HR. If you add your resting HR, they switch to the Karvonen method (% of heart rate reserve), which fits your fitness better. Weight is only used for calories. The screen refreshes at the rate you pick next to the buttons (default 5 s) with the average of that window; readings are recorded every second.
